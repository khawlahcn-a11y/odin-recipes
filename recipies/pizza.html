Pizza Dough Recipie

An easy pizza crust that makes an exceptional homemade pizza – puffy crust with a chewy, flavourful crumb inside.
Enough structure to pick up, but not thin and stiff like a cracker

.

Ingredients

  • 3 1/2 cups (440g) all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons (one packet) active dry yeast
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 1 1/2 cups (360ml) warm water (110°F/45°C)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus more for greasing the bowl
