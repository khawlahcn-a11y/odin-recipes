Cinnamon Rolls Recipie

Soft, fluffy cinnamon rolls made from scratch with a dump-and-mix dough that requires no kneading! Frosted with a sticky cream cheese glaze, these are sweet but nowhere near as sickly sweet as store bought ones.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (240ml) whole milk, warmed to 110°F/45°C
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons (one packet) active dry yeast
  • 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
  • 1/3 cup (75g) unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 4 cups (500g) all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
  • 1 cup (200g) packed light brown sugar
  • 3 tablespoons ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup (115g) unsalted butter, softened
  • 8 ounces (225g) cream cheese, softened
  • 1 1/2 cups (190g) powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
